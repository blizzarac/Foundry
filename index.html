IRONHEX

A turn-based hex roguelike in a dead machine world.
Every attack is telegraphed. Every failure is yours.

You are a salvaged combat frame, five sectors deep in a foundry that never shut down. Everything still running down here wants you for parts.
Tap a hex to move or strike. Red hexes are where machines will hit next cycle — do not be standing there.
Power drives strikes, dashes and deflects; moving and venting restore it.
Deflect an adjacent attacker as it swings to overload it — then counterstrike for double. Hit an exposed core from behind for critical damage.
Repair bays restore integrity, refill cells and sell upgrades — but docking reinitializes the sector, and everything respawns.
Loot drops in grades: Normal, Magic (up to 2 modifiers), Rare (up to 4) and a few Uniques. Equip one weapon, plating, sensor, drive and utility module each.
Currency orbs from caches and elite machines craft your gear — transmute, augment, alchemy, regal, exalt, chaos. Swapping or crafting is free — unless hostiles have you in sensor range, then it costs your turn.
Fall, and your cores scatter where you died. Your next frame can reclaim them from the wreck.
Beat the OVERSEER and the Foundry opens: an endless overworld of keyed sector nodes that grows as you purge it. Your frame, gear and map persist between sessions.
Some nodes carry an Anomaly (⚒ ◫ ▸ ☣) — a fabricator to survive, a vault racing a lockdown, a convoy to cut off, or a corrupted pocket worth the risk.
Hover or hold on any machine to read its attack pattern.
