EMBERHEX

A turn-based hex roguelike. Every strike is telegraphed.
Every death is your own.

Tap a hex to step or strike. Red hexes are where enemies will hit next turn — don't be there.
Stamina fuels attacks, rolls and parries; stepping and waiting restore it.
Parry an adjacent attacker as it strikes to stagger it — then make it pay. Strike from behind for backstabs.
Bonfires heal, refill your flask and sell upgrades — but the floor stirs back to life.
Die, and your souls stain the floor where you fell. The next you may reclaim them.
